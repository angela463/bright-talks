Bright Talks courses

Bright Talks is here for every family that wants honest, caring guidance. You don’t have to start at lesson one or follow a rigid path—jump in where it fits your child’s age and stage, whether you’re curious about one topic or building habits over time.

Each lesson is under ten minutes, so you can press play, listen, and learn right alongside your child. We keep things clear, practical, and genuinely fun, so conversations feel lighter and you walk away with words you can use right away.
