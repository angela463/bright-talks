  • Heidi Cooper

    Instructor, Bright Talks

    Foundations: Early Years

    Hi, I’m Heidi. I’ve spent years walking alongside parents who want to do this well, but feel unsure where to start when a child asks a body question at the dinner table or in the car.

    I lead Foundations: Early Years, Bright Talks’ first course: short, warm lessons that give you practical language for everyday moments, not a lecture you have to get through in one sitting.

    My work centers on shame-free language, parent-led pacing, and the belief that trust grows in small, honest answers.

    “Trust grows in the small answers. When you respond calmly to the little questions, your child learns they can come to you with the big ones.”

HONEST FIT

Is this course for you?

We’d rather you skip it than regret it. Here’s who it’s built for, and who it isn’t, yet.

✓ For you if…

  • Your child is roughly 3 to 7, and curious in ways you didn’t expect this early.
  • You want calm, dignity-first language, never clinical and never fearful.
  • You have ten minutes a week you can protect for this.
  • You want to stop stalling on the small conversations before they pile up.

× Maybe not yet if…

  • Your child is 13+. Our Teen Corner course will fit better.
  • You’re looking for a single, one-time “big talk”. This is built for ongoing.
  • You prefer clinical or academic tone. We lean warm and plain-spoken.
