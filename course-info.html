Hi, I'm Priya.

DR. PRIYA MALHOTRA · RESEARCH AND EDUCATION ADVISOR, BRIGHT TALKS

I spent years in early-childhood settings watching kind, capable parents quietly panic when their preschooler asked a body question at the grocery store. Not because they didn't know the answer, but because no one had ever taught them how to answer calmly.

This course is what I wish had existed for those parents. It's short on purpose. It's respectful on purpose. And it never asks you to sit through ninety minutes of theory when what you really need is a three-sentence answer for bath-time tonight.

Every lesson you finish is one less moment your child has to find the answer somewhere else first.

"Safety isn't one talk. It's the way you answer every small question before it becomes a big one."
