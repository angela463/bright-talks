Hi, I'm Paige. I spent years in early-childhood settings watching capable parents freeze when a preschooler asked a body question out of nowhere—not because they didn't know, but because no one had shown them how to answer calmly.

We’d rather you skip it than regret it. Here’s who it’s built for, and who it isn’t, yet.

✓ For you if…

  • Your child is roughly 3 to 7, and curious in ways you didn’t expect this early.
  • You want calm, dignity-first language, never clinical and never fearful.
  • You have ten minutes a week you can protect for this.
  • You want to stop stalling on the small conversations before they pile up.

× Maybe not yet if…

  • Your child is 13+. Our Teen Corner course will fit better.
