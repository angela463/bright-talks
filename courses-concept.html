    How the course journey works

    Bright Talks is built for real homes—busy schedules, curious questions, and the hope that your child will always know they can come to you.

    1. Watch the welcome See how talks are structured and what to expect.
    2. Open a talk Short video, parent guidance, and optional audio narration.
    3. Use the scripts Take phrases and handbooks into everyday moments.
    4. Return anytime Small, repeated conversations matter more than one big talk.
